5. CRITICAL DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED DURING DEVICE VERIFICATION
======================================================================
Defect (live on Infinix X6731, discovered by reading the device's durable work table):
- 6 of 24 scheduled reminders (a weekly Friday-09:15 series) had their target moved to 06:00 local (the Quiet Hours end) even though 09:15 − 10 = 09:05 is OUTSIDE the quiet window (00:00–06:00). delayUntilEnd had fired incorrectly.
Root cause:
- ReminderQuietHours.delayUntilEnd was called WITHOUT the canonical device tz.Location. It then falls back to DateTime.toLocal(), whose default zone binding in a headless WorkManager isolate can differ from the app isolate, producing a quiet-end on the wrong wall clock.
Fix:
- ReminderReconciler gained deviceLocation (tz.Location?) and passes it into BOTH quiet-hours evaluations (proposed target + inexact delivery window).
- IanaCalendarEventTimeZones gained deviceLocation (canonical device zone from the SAME source Event/Task scheduling uses).
- Headless runtime passes zones.deviceLocation; the app root overrides the new reminderDeviceLocationProvider (notification_providers.dart) so the main-isolate reconciler uses the identical zone.
